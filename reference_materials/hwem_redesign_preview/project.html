PROPOSED GHITORNI FACILITY

A cleaner energy future, planned in New Delhi.

A proposed 3.5 TPD compressed biogas initiative using segregated organic waste and cattle manure as feedstock.

Purpose-built for resource recovery.

The proposed project in Ghitorni, New Delhi, would include segregated waste handling, preparation, mesophilic anaerobic digestion, gas upgrading and digestate processing.

Its viability depends on feasibility work, reliable feedstock supply, applicable standards and approvals, grid connectivity and commercially viable offtake arrangements.

Target output3.5 TPD CBG (proposed)
Site conceptApprox. 4 acres (described in proposal)
Collection radiusWithin approx. 15 km (planned)

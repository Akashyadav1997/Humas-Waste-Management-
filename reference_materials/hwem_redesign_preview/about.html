WHO WE ARE

Recovering value from what we leave behind.

Our mission connects better management of biodegradable organic waste with cleaner energy and more sustainable farming.

A circular vision for urban waste.

Humus Waste & Energy Management proposes to turn segregated organic waste, kitchen waste and cattle manure into renewable biogas and organic fertilizer products.

This approach supports circular resource use by considering waste a feedstock rather than an endpoint. The company intends to explore a proposed renewable-energy facility in Ghitorni, New Delhi.

01 / RESOURCE

Waste recovery

Build responsible sourcing relationships for segregated biodegradable waste.

02 / ENERGY

Renewable fuel

Develop biogas production and upgrading using proposed anaerobic digestion technology.

03 / AGRICULTURE

Organic inputs

Recover value from digestate to support soil-friendly agricultural practices.

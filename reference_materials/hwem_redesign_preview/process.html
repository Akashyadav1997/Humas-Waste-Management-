PROCESS OVERVIEW

From organic material to useful resources.

A proposed process combining feedstock preparation, biological digestion, gas upgrading and organic manure recovery.

One input stream. Two useful outcomes.

Segregated kitchen, fruit and vegetable waste and cattle manure are prepared and fed into anaerobic digestion. Biogas is then upgraded and conditioned to relevant standards. Digestate follows a separate processing pathway for organic fertilizers.

STAGE 01

Receive & prepare

Collection, segregation, shredding and mixing.

STAGE 02

Digest & recover

Mesophilic anaerobic digestion creates raw biogas and digestate.

STAGE 03

Upgrade & process

Gas purification and digestate processing create separate resource streams.
